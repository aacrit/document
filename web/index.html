The Partition of India

1946 to 1948, told with the public record

This film tells of killing, abduction and flight through the words of people who were there. Every reading is word for word from its source, sometimes in part (marked ...). Voices are read by a computer; no recording is imitated. This film shows no bodies or killing. Every second carries a mark that says what it is.

Film and photographs from the National Archives, the Internet Archive and Wikimedia Commons. Words from Hansard and the Punjab Boundary Commission papers.
