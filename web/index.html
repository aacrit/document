The Partition of India

1946 to today, told with the public record

Two nations were born in the same week of August 1947. On the night of 14 August, in Delhi, Nehru told the Constituent Assembly that not far from Delhi, big cities were ablaze. A narrator tells it as one story, with readings from the record: the clock, the line, the two midnights, the roads beside the canals, Delhi itself, and those who stayed.

It tells of killing, abduction and flight. Every reading is word for word from its source, sometimes in part (marked ...). This film shows no bodies or killing. Every shot carries a mark that says what it is.
