What the marks mean

The mark in the corner says what you see.

ARCHIVEthe archive and year

Real film or photograph from the archive, unaltered beyond trims, crops and slow moves.

RECONSTRUCTED{{legend_reconstructed}}

Archive film or photographs changed in a way that matters: colourised, re-timed, stabilised, or enlarged by a computer model. The mark names the change.

AI-GENERATEDcontext

Made by a computer model: places, weather, objects. Never people, never a recorded event.

DRAWN{{legend_drawn}}

A map or diagram drawn by us from named data.

VOID DOCUMENT

Text on black: titles, chapter cards, and where the record is silent.
