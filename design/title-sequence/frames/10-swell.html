Artifact
A rebbelib (Library of Congress 2010586182, 1920s): a Marshallese stick chart of both the Ratak and Ralik chains. Shells mark the atolls, straight sticks the regular currents, curved sticks the swells, and typed paper strips with atoll names are glued to its sticks. Below it, Admiralty chart 983, Marshall Islands (published 1891, the new edition of 1931).
