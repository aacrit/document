void document · As It Was

The archive machine: the world, made only of its records, turning. One camera through a clockwork of the instruments people recorded their world with, from a Sumerian labour register to the Lumière cinématographe, lit by one lamp. Then the machine settles on this film's own record, and the model gives way to the real scan.
