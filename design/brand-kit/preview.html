4. The springs, felt

Each bar fills on one preset for exactly its settle time. The last bar is the rostrum curve for an 8 s Ken Burns move, compressed to 2 s: it ramps, cruises, and stops.

5. The demo strip

Every 5th frame of a 6 s render from motion.py at 24 fps: a flagged lower third with a raised cue and the watermark settling, a cut with a class crossfade, a dip to black, a chapter card. Regenerate with python design/brand-kit/motion.py.
